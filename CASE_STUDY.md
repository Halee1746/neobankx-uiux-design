# NeoBank X Case Study

## Problem

Users need a simple banking app to send money, manage cards, and track spending.

## Research

I researched modern banking apps and FinTech dashboards.

## Personas

Created 3 personas:

* College Student
* Working Professional
* Small Business Owner

## User Flows

Created flows for:

* Login
* Send Money
* Analytics
* Card Management

## Wireframes

Created wireframes for all major screens.

## Final UI

Designed onboarding, login, dashboard, analytics, card management, and send money screens.

## Accessibility

Used readable text, proper spacing, and clear buttons.

## Challenges

Creating the design system and prototype.

## Future Improvements

Add AI insights and voice banking.
